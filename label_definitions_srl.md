## Core participants

### Agent
The participant that deliberately performs, initiates or controls the event (who does it). Example: the ruler or company that captures people.

### Patient
The participant that is affected by, undergoes, or is the object of the event (to whom or what it is done). Example: the people who are captured.

### AgentPatient
A participant that is both agent and patient of the same event: it acts and is itself affected, e.g. reflexive or reciprocal events, or a participant who moves or changes state of their own accord.

### Benefactive
The participant for whose benefit (or harm) the event is carried out, without being the direct patient. Example: the recipient of a gift, or the party on whose behalf something is done.

## Means and content

### Instrument
The tool, means, or vehicle used by the agent to carry out the event (e.g. a ship used to attack, a letter used to inform).

### Cargo
The goods, people, or things that are carried, shipped, or transported as part of the event, when they are not simply the patient of a capture or attack (e.g. rice loaded on a ship).

## Circumstance

### Time
The time, date, period, or duration in which the event takes place, including the introducing word when it is part of the time expression (e.g. "sedert t. Jaer 1631").

### Location
The place where the event takes place, without implying movement.

## Spatial movement

### Source
The place or entity from which movement, transfer, or departure starts.

### Target
The place or entity toward which movement, transfer, or action is directed, or where it ends up.

### Path
The route, passage, or intermediate place through or along which movement takes place (e.g. a strait, a coast).
